The show

A sisterhood with the door unlocked.

Shape Up Sister is a weekly podcast about getting stronger in a body that has a life attached to it. The room is held for women and people who identify as women. Anyone interested is welcome to listen, learn, and stay.

Why “sister”

Not a blood test. A posture.

Sister means someone who will tell you the truth about the program and also save you a seat. If you are a woman, if you identify as a woman, or if you simply want to understand this conversation better, you belong in the headphones.

Who it’s for

If any of these are you, start at episode one — or twelve.

Women

Cis women who want strength and a less punishing story about food.

People who identify as women

Trans women and anyone who names themselves in this room.

Anyone interested

Partners, friends, trainers, family. Listen and learn.
